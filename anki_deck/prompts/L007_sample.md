# Task: example sentences for Japanese flashcards

You are writing example sentences for a learner's Anki deck (Kanji in Context). Write **2 sentences for each of the 10 target cards below — 20 sentences in total**, all in this one reply.

## Target cards

|---|---|---|---|

| 357 | 倍 | ばい | double, twice, two times |
| 360 | 全く | まったく | quite, entirely, thoroughly, completely |
| 363 | 間、間 | あいだ、ま | あいだ: interval, space, gap, distance; ま: time, interval, room |
| 366 | 回る | まわる | to turn (round), to go (round), to revolve, to rotate |
| 370 | 大体 | だいたい | on the whole, by and large, roughly, for the most part, main (points) |
| 372 | 先頭 | せんとう | the forefront, the head, the lead, the top |
| 381 | ～足、～足 | ～そく、～ぞく | ～ pair(s) of (shoes, socks, etc.) |
| 383 | 足す | たす | to add, to supply |
| 387 | 全力で | ぜんりょくで | with all one's might, to the best of one's ability |

For 間、間 write one sentence with あいだ and one with ま. For ～足、～足 the word is a counter for pairs of shoes/socks (一足 いっそく, 二足 にそく, 三足 さんぞく, 何足 なんぞく).

## Rules

1. **Use the target word in the sense given in the Meaning column**, with exactly the reading given (人[ひと] is not 人[にん]). Verbs and adjectives may be conjugated.
2. **Vocabulary.** The learner knows the words in the list at the end, i.e. every card at or before the target card's deck position. Each sentence may contain **at most 2 kanji words that are not in that list**, and those must be common, everyday words (no idioms, proverbs, literary or rare words). A list word combined freely with another list word is NOT known (山 and 水 are known, 山水 is not). Conjugated forms of list words count as known. Words normally written in kana are free.
3. **Write normal Japanese.** Use kanji where Japanese normally would. Do not replace kanji with kana to avoid unknown words.
4. **Short and natural.** At most about 22 characters. Everyday situations, intermediate grammar, something a native speaker would actually say or write.
5. **Variety.** The two sentences for a card must use different situations and different vocabulary, and no sentence pattern may repeat across cards.
6. **Furigana format.** Put every word that contains kanji in brackets as `[kanji|reading]`, one bracket per word, reading in hiragana, okurigana outside the bracket: `[今日|きょう]は[雨|あめ]が[降|ふ]っている。` Every kanji in the sentence must be inside a bracket. The target word gets its own bracket(s), not merged with neighbouring words.
7. **English.** A natural English translation of the whole sentence.

## Output

Output exactly one code block of JSON Lines, one sentence per line, 20 lines, in the order of the table, nothing else inside the block:

```jsonl
{"card": "<word exactly as in the Word column>", "ja": "<bracketed sentence>", "en": "<English>", "new_words": ["<kanji words used that are not in the list>"]}
```

After the code block, write one short line per sentence you were unsure about (reading, naturalness, or vocabulary), or "No doubts."
